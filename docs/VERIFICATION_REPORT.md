## One-Cycle Evidence

Multiply/divide, CSR, compressed, bitmanip, and non-memory execute paths retire after fetch without iterative helper state; AXI4 bus waits, AMO exclusive retries, and Decoupled debug-retire backpressure are modeled as front-end/LSU/debug wait states.
